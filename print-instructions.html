📄 Resume PDF Generation Instructions

Method 1: Browser Print (Recommended)

Step 1: Open the resume in your browser:
Open Resume
Step 2: Press Ctrl+P (Windows) or Cmd+P (Mac) to open print dialog
Step 3: Configure print settings:
  • Destination: Save as PDF
  • Pages: All
  • Layout: Portrait
  • Paper size: A4
  • Margins: Default
  • Options: ✅ Background graphics
Step 4: Click "Save" and choose filename: niels-uitterdijk-resume.pdf

Method 2: Browser Developer Tools (Advanced)

Step 1: Open resume and press F12 to open Developer Tools
Step 2: Press Ctrl+Shift+P to open command palette
Step 3: Type "print" and select "Rendering > Emulate CSS media type > print"
Step 4: This shows exactly how the PDF will look, then use Ctrl+P to print
